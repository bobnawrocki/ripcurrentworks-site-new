Tell Baxter what you know. Baxter helps shape what the trip should become.

Baxter is built for the messy middle of travel planning, when you have a few facts, a few hopes, and a lot of decisions still ahead.

  • Plan better tripsPlan with Baxter is guided discovery, not generic itinerary generation. Start with a marathon, wedding, conference, flight deal, road trip, or loose idea, then let Baxter help organize the next useful questions.
  • Built around real detailsTrips, travelers, preferences, documents, lodging, dining, activities, and notes have a structured place to live.

Travel with confidence.

During travel, Baxter’s Today view keeps the important pieces visible without turning the trip into a spreadsheet.

  • Today viewSee upcoming reservations, current destination context, practical nearby essentials, and trip readiness cues in one calm place.
  • Current contextMove through the day with the details that matter now, while the rest of the trip remains organized in the background.

Keep traveler details organized.

Baxter gives each traveler a profile for the information that can shape better recommendations and smoother planning.

  • Profiles and preferencesKeep travel style, dining preferences, pace, loyalty programs, and Known Traveler Number context close to each trip.
  • Readiness contextTrack passport and important document notes without making claims Baxter cannot guarantee. The goal is practical organization, not false certainty.

Recommendations that fit you.

Baxter is designed to understand the difference between a polished hotel bar, a quick family stop, a local favorite, and a familiar chain when that is what the moment calls for.

  • Lodging, dining, and activitiesFind options that match the trip, the traveler, the destination, and the day.
  • Travel style mattersRecommendations can reflect pace, local character, comfort level, and the kind of experience you actually want.

Built for real trips.

Some trips are simple. Some have multiple cities, anchors, detours, and half-formed ideas. Baxter is being shaped for both.

  • Single-city or multi-cityPlan one destination, a connected route, or a trip that changes shape as you learn more.
  • Anchored and exploratorySupport trips built around fixed events, road trips, seasonal ideas, and early drafts that are not ready to become an itinerary yet.
